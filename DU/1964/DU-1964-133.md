Minister Spraw Zagranicznych: w z. J. Winiewicz de Finlande sur l'abrogation de la Convention entre la République de Pologne et la République de Finlande relalive a lexploitation des lignes de communication aćrienne róguliere, signee a Helsinki le 29 Juillet 1938, Protocole dont la teneur suit: :

PROTOCOL.

between the People's Republic of Poland and the Republic of Finland concerning termination of Convention between the Republic of Poland and the Republic of Finland concerning the operation of seheduled air services, signed at Helsinki on July 29, 1938.

The People's Republic of Poland and the Republic of Finland have agreed as follows:

Article 1

Convention between the Republic of Poland and the Republic of Finland concerning the operation: of scheduled air services, signed at Helsinki on July 29, 1938, is terminated by this Protocol.

Article 2

The present Protocol is subject to ratification and will come into force on the day of exchange of the instruments of ratification which will take place in Warsaw.

Done at Helsinki this 4th day of November, 1963, in duplicate, in the English language, both texts having the same authenticity.

For the People's Republic For the Republic of Finland of Poland E. Pietkiewicz W. Schreck
